# A spec uses no -ing form as a noun or an adjective

A spec must not use an "-ing" form as a noun or an adjective where a different form is possible.

Reason: ASD-STE100 permits an "-ing" form only where no different form is possible
(spec 000025).

Test: `spezi check` gives a warning for an "-ing" word after an article (spec 000093).
