# A spec is written in ASD-STE100

A spec must be in English and must follow these rules of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical
English:

- Use at most 20 words in an instruction and at most 25 words in a description.
- Use the active voice.
- Write one instruction in one sentence.
- Use one word for one thing.
- Do not use "-ing" forms as nouns or adjectives where a different form is possible.

Code, names, paths and commands keep their own form.

Reason: Short and fixed sentences are clear to every reader and to every agent.
