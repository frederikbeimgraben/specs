# A spec is written in ASD-STE100

A spec must follow the rules of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English in
(spec 000102, 000103, 000104, 000105, 000106, 000107, 000108).
Code, names, paths and commands in a spec keep their own form.

Reason: Short and fixed sentences are clear to every reader and to every agent.
